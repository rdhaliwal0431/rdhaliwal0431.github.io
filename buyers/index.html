The steps

  1. Talk to a lender. A mortgage pre-approval sets your budget and helps your offer.
  2. Set your search. We agree on areas, home types and must-haves, and I set up listing alerts.
  3. View homes. I book showings and point out questions to ask.
  4. Make an offer. I explain price, deposit, conditions (financing, inspection) and possession dates in plain language.
  5. Remove conditions. Your inspector, lender and lawyer do their parts; I coordinate the timeline.
  6. Possession. Your lawyer completes the transfer and you get the keys.
