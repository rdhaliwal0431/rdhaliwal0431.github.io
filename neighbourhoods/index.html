Winnipeg neighbourhood guides

General notes to help you narrow your search. Ask me for current listings and recent sales in any area.

Ramjit Dhaliwal, REALTOR® | Real Broker Manitoba Ltd.

Downtown Winnipeg

The city's business core, with offices, condos, the Exchange District's heritage buildings and easy transit connections. Popular with professionals and businesses that want a central address.

St. Boniface

Winnipeg's historic French quarter across the Red River from downtown, with a mix of older homes, newer infill, local shops and commercial streets.

CentrePort Canada area

An inland port and industrial area in the northwest of the city, with access to rail, highways and the airport. A focus for warehouse, distribution and industrial land.

Bridgwater

A newer community in southwest Winnipeg with parks, walking paths and a town centre with shops and services. Mostly detached homes, plus some townhouses and condos.

South St. Vital

Established and newer residential streets in the southeast, near shopping, schools and parks, with a range of detached homes and condos.

North Kildonan

A residential area along Henderson Highway with mature trees, river views in places and a mix of bungalows, bi-levels and two-storey homes.
